## Profile (2026-11-20, step 2.1)

Tool: callgrind (instruction counts, Ir). These are deterministic, so they double as a noise-free benchmark metric. perf sampling is unavailable under this WSL kernel (`perf_event_paranoid` = 2, and changing it needs root). Build config: `profile` / `profile_fire` (default `-O2 -flto` plus `-g`; outputs bitwise identical to `default`). Command: `regress.sh profile <case>`; annotated output goes to `$WORK/profile/`. With LTO inlining, some costs are attributed to the caller's file. The old untracked `callgrind.out`/`gmon.out` (Sep 28) are superseded.

**`ward_1m`** (Ward, 1 month, about 11.6k patches; 87.9 G Ir; 22 s native, 13 min under callgrind):

| Share | What |
|---|---|
| 40% | **initialization**: `construct_routing_topology` 33%, mostly `compute_transmissivity_curve` (30%): 60 M `compute_field_capacity` calls and 120 M `compute_z_final` calls; worldfile parsing (`fscanf`/`sscanf`) about 7%. A fixed cost: about 4% of a 1-yr run and <0.5% of `ward_long` |
| 60% | **simulation** (1.7 G Ir/day). Of total: subsurface routing `hillslope_daily_F` → `update_drainage_land` 29% (8.6 M calls = 24 routing steps/day × patches); `compute_N_leached` 6.6% (4 calls per drainage call, one per solute); `compute_z_final` 5.5%; `compute_field_capacity` (daily, via layer field capacity) about 6%; output filter `output_basin` 2% |
| about 30% | libm `pow`/`exp` (spread over the above: field capacity, z_final, N leaching, transmissivity) |

**`w8_fire_nc`** (W8 + WMFire + netcdf clim, 10 yr; 115.0 G Ir): `update_drainage_land` 33% inclusive (20.2 M calls); `pow`/`exp` 30%; **`output_basin` 8.0%** (3 basin filters, so output costs more on a small basin); **`canopy_stratum_hourly` calloc/free 4.8%** (21.6 M calloc+free pairs, one per stratum per hour); `compute_N_leached` + `compute_z_final` 10%; `compute_field_capacity` 6%. WMFire itself is not in the top 25.
